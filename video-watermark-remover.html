Why video watermarks are harder — and why most tools fail

Removing a watermark from a photo is one inpainting pass. A video is thousands of photos: the mark has to be erased from every single frame, consistently, without flicker — while the audio stays in sync and the file doesn't balloon in size. That's why most "free video watermark remover" sites either cap you at 10 seconds, crush your video to 480p, or quietly upload your footage to their servers and make you wait in a render queue.

ClearMark does the whole job on your own device. The AI model runs in your browser via WebAssembly (WebGPU when available), painting over the masked watermark area frame by frame. Your video never leaves the tab — which also means no queues, no accounts, and no surprise "premium export" paywall. The trade-off is honest: processing speed depends on your hardware, but short clips typically finish in under a minute.

Remove a video watermark in 3 steps

  1. Upload your video. Open the ClearMark editor and drop in your MP4, MOV or WebM file (up to 100 MB). It never leaves your browser.
  2. Mask the watermark. Draw a rectangle or brush over the logo or text overlay. If the mark moves, mask each position it appears in — one mask set covers the whole timeline.
  3. Process and download. Click "Remove watermark", preview the cleaned frames, and download the result — original resolution, frame rate and audio untouched.
