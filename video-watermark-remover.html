Watermarks ruin good footage

A logo in the corner. A text overlay across the bottom. A bug that drifts mid-video. Whatever the mark, it turns usable footage into something you can't ship — and re-shooting isn't an option.

ClearMark applies your mask across the timeline: static marks are cleaned in one pass, moving marks get masked per position. The AI repaints only the marked pixels; resolution, frame rate and audio stay exactly as exported.

Questions, answered

How can I remove a watermark from a video for free?

Upload your MP4, MOV or WebM to ClearMark, mask the watermark area, and let the AI repaint it frame by frame. Free, no sign-up, processed in your browser.

Which video formats are supported?

MP4, MOV and WebM up to 100 MB. The cleaned video keeps the original resolution, frame rate and audio.

What if the watermark moves during the video?

Mask each position where the watermark appears. ClearMark applies your masks across the timeline and cleans every frame.

How long does video watermark removal take?

It depends on video length and your device — short clips finish in under a minute. Processing runs on your own hardware via WebAssembly/WebGPU.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser tab. Your footage never leaves your device.

Tip: only remove watermarks from media you created or own. Reposting other creators’ content without credit violates platform terms.
