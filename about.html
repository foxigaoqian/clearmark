ClearMark

Free Watermark Remover — Clean Images & Videos in Your Browser. Nothing to install. Nothing to sign up for.

v1.0.0 MIT License No telemetry No accounts No cloud

🎯 Why this exists

Most online watermark removers charge $10–$30 / month, upload your private images to unknown servers, and slap their own watermark on the free tier. That's broken.

ClearMark runs entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves the tab. No subscriptions, no accounts, no cloud — verifiable because the source is MIT-licensed and the network tab is empty after first load.

Use it to clean up watermarks on your own photos and videos — AI-generated images, social clips, drafts with leftover marks. Please respect other people's copyrights and platform terms.

⚙️ How it works (the boring honest version)

  1. You drop an image or video in. The file stays in-memory in your browser. It never leaves.
  2. You paint the watermark. Pick a preset for common AI watermarks (Gemini, DALL·E, Midjourney…), or draw your own mask — that becomes the inpainting mask.
  3. An AI model runs in WebAssembly. The model (LaMa, ~50 MB, downloaded once and cached) runs inside your browser tab via ONNX Runtime Web — on your GPU if your browser supports WebGPU, otherwise on your CPU.
  4. You download the result. Same browser, same tab, same machine. No server saw a thing.

Open your browser's Network tab to verify: after the first load, no requests are made.

💝 Free & open source

ClearMark is free forever. There are no servers to pay for, no subscriptions to add later, no premium tier to unlock.

Based on open-source WatermarkOut (MIT). The inpainting engine is LaMa (Suvorov et al., 2021), running via ONNX Runtime Web.

🔧 Built with

  • Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no framework, no node_modules
  • LaMa — Large Mask Inpainting (Suvorov et al., 2021)
  • ONNX Runtime Web — WebGPU + WASM AI inference in the browser
  • IndexedDB + Service Worker — offline support
