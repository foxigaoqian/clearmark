When the YouTube watermark gets in the way

YouTube stamps its logo on Shorts and embeds a branding watermark on videos viewed or shared through its players. That's fine on YouTube itself — but when you reuse your own footage elsewhere (your portfolio site, a pitch deck, TikTok, a course platform), the YouTube badge makes the content look borrowed instead of owned. Re-editing from the project file isn't always possible: sometimes the original project is long gone, or you're working with an exported copy.

Desktop video editors can clone-stamp a logo out, but that's slow, frame-by-frame manual work — and most "free YouTube watermark remover" websites just upload your video to their servers and hand back a compressed copy with their watermark on it. ClearMark instead runs an AI inpainting model locally in your browser: mask the logo once, and it's erased across every frame while resolution, frame rate and audio stay exactly as they were.

Remove a YouTube watermark in 3 steps

  1. Upload your video. Open the ClearMark editor and drop in your MP4, MOV or WebM file (up to 100 MB). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  2. Mask the watermark. Draw a rectangle over the YouTube logo or branding overlay. Since it sits in a fixed corner, one mask covers the whole timeline.
  3. Process and download. Click "Remove watermark", check the preview, and download the clean video — original quality, audio in sync.
