Questions, answered

Can I remove the YouTube watermark from my own downloaded videos?

Yes. Upload your MP4, MOV or WebM file to ClearMark, mask the YouTube logo or branding overlay, and download the cleaned video at original quality.

Does it work on YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Shorts carry the same corner watermark — mask it once and ClearMark cleans it across every frame.

Is there a file size limit?

Files up to 100 MB are supported. For best results use the original export rather than a re-compressed copy.

Will the audio stay in sync?

Yes. ClearMark preserves the original audio track untouched while only the watermarked pixels are repainted.

Do I need an account or to install software?

Neither. It runs in your browser with no sign-up, and works offline after the first visit.
