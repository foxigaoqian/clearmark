Questions, answered

How do I remove the Instagram watermark from a Reel I downloaded?

Upload the Reel to ClearMark, paint over the Instagram logo with the brush or rectangle tool, and download the cleaned video. Works for Reels, Stories and feed videos.

Is the Instagram watermark remover really free?

Yes. ClearMark is free forever with no account required. Processing happens in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Can I remove the watermark from Instagram photos too?

Yes — JPG, PNG, WEBP and BMP photos are supported alongside MP4, MOV and WebM videos.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. ClearMark is a web app: open it, drop your file in, and you're done. It even works offline after your first visit.

Is it safe to upload my Reels here?

There is nothing to upload — files are processed locally in your browser tab and never transmitted anywhere.

Tip: only remove watermarks from media you created or own. Reposting other creators’ content without credit violates platform terms.
