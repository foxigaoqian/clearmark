The problem with the Instagram watermark

When you download your own Reel from Instagram, it arrives with the Instagram logo and your handle burned into the video. Posting that file anywhere else looks second-hand: the watermark tells every viewer (and every platform's ranking algorithm) that this is recycled content. TikTok and YouTube both quietly suppress videos carrying a competitor's watermark, so your own clip underperforms for no good reason.

Common workarounds all have costs. Screen-recording before posting loses quality. Cropping chops your composition. Third-party "Instagram watermark remover" sites make you upload your footage to strangers' servers and often stamp their own watermark on the free tier. ClearMark takes a different path: the AI inpainting runs entirely in your browser tab. Your Reel never leaves your device, the result keeps original quality, and there's no account or paywall standing between you and a clean file.
