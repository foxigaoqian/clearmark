Why the TikTok watermark is annoying

Every video you save from TikTok comes stamped with the TikTok logo and your username — and the logo bounces between corners throughout the clip. That's fine inside TikTok, but the moment you cross-post your own content to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or a client presentation, the watermark screams "ripped from TikTok" and other platforms quietly deprioritize watermarked reposts. Cropping it out ruins your framing; blurring it looks worse. The clean fix is to remove it and let AI repaint the pixels underneath.

ClearMark does exactly that, with one big difference from the usual "TikTok watermark remover" sites: your file never leaves your browser. Most free removers upload your video to a server farm (slow, and who knows what happens to your footage). ClearMark runs the AI inpainting model locally via WebAssembly — private by architecture, not by promise.

How to remove a TikTok watermark in 3 steps

  1. Drop in your video or photo. Open the ClearMark editor and upload your MP4, MOV, WebM, JPG or PNG (up to 100 MB). It stays on your device.
  2. Mark the watermark. Use the rectangle or brush tool to paint over the TikTok logo. Because the logo moves, mask each corner position it appears in — the tool processes the video frame by frame.
  3. Remove and download. Hit "Remove watermark", let the AI fill the masked areas (usually 5–30 seconds), preview the result, and download it at original quality — resolution, frame rate and audio untouched.
