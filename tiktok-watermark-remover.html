The bouncing logo problem

Every video you save from TikTok comes stamped with the logo and your username — and the logo bounces between corners for the whole clip. Inside TikTok that's fine. The moment you cross-post your own content to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or a client deck, the watermark marks it as a repost — and other platforms quietly deprioritize watermarked uploads.

Cropping it out ruins your framing. Blurring it looks worse. The clean fix is to remove the logo and let AI repaint the pixels underneath — which is exactly what ClearMark does, with one difference from the usual remover sites: your file never leaves your browser.
